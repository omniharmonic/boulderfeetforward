Every Tuesday at 2:30 PM, we host our flagship outreach event in Boulder's Central Park. Since 2020, it has supplied a nutritious meal with a full serving of protein and vegetables, plus snacks to go, water, personal care, basic first aid, and sexual health supplies. With help from volunteers and groups across the community, we can also offer a second meal to take for later, pet care supplies and food, clothing, socks, shoes, gear, and a device charging station.

Our goal isn't only that our unhoused neighbors eat a wholesome meal, although that's good enough for us. We want to create a welcoming space where people can have as many needs met as possible in one place.

Tuesdays in the Park is a gathering place for our partner providers, who connect with people here: Focus ReEntry, Community Court, Recovery Café, TGTHR Youth Shelters, Mental Health Partners, and others. We proudly host the Works team from Boulder County Public Health, who bring harm reduction and safe-use supplies and wound care kits. We often have guest tables from agencies like SPAN and Urban Peak. Our resource navigators are on site to answer questions, make referrals, and provide Coordinated Entry and housing assessments.

Each week we reach about 85 people at this event. It's more than a distribution. People can stock up on supplies, and they can also find real connection and get the help they need to build a path to stability.
