We launched Friday Breakfast Club in 2024, with coffee and breakfast sandwiches every Friday at 11:00 AM in Central Park. Since then it has grown to include our community partners and volunteer groups, who add a lunch for later, fresh fruit and snacks, clothing, gear, socks, a charging station, and our usual hygiene items.

We hold this event earlier in the day as a way into continued participation. Right after Friday Breakfast Club, we offer drop-in hours: work with us from where you are, whether that's moving your case management forward, troubleshooting something that just came up, or simply talking as peers.

With help from volunteer community experts, our Friday meals are often followed by workshops or special events. We've hosted the ACLU for a Know Your Rights talk, along with yoga, poetry and art workshops, board game days and tournaments, yard games, and free movies from the Boulder Public Library.

As with Tuesdays in the Park, we're not just feeding people. We're bringing our unhoused neighbors into the broader community, with zero-barrier chances to learn, play, take care of themselves, and express themselves.
