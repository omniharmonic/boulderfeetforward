Our weekly events run on volunteers. In 2024 alone, people gave about 424 hours at our Tuesday events, and that doesn't count Fridays. It's the difference between a table of supplies and a place where people feel welcome.

There's a job for every comfort level: setting up and breaking down, serving meals, handing out supplies, keeping the charging station running, and simply being friendly and present. Groups are welcome, and so are people who've never done outreach before. Our team will show you the ropes at the start of every shift.

If you have a skill to share, we'd love to hear it. Yoga, poetry, art, board games, and know-your-rights talks have all been part of our Friday events.

**Volunteers earn bus passes.** Anyone who volunteers three or more hours a week at our events, or at events like ours, can receive a monthly bus pass. That includes our neighbors who volunteer with us.
